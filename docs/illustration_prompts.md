# Phase 5 — Illustration prompts (Chapter 22)

Paste one prompt per image into your image AI. Every prompt ends with the same style block.
Send the images back; each is checked for resolution, background, stray text and accuracy
before it goes into the site (compressed WebP + a "what to notice" caption).

**Style block (identical on every prompt):**
> Style: clean modern medical illustration, soft semi-realistic shading, muted clinical palette of soft reds, warm skin tones, pale yellow for urine and teal accents, even studio lighting. One single illustration only, never a grid, collage or contact sheet. At least 1600 pixels on the long side. Plain pure white background with no checkerboard pattern and no shadow box. The whole subject is fully visible with generous empty margins; nothing is cut off at any edge. Absolutely no text, labels, letters, numbers, arrows with writing, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.
