# Phase 5: Illustration prompts (Chapter 25)

Paste one prompt per image into your image AI. Every prompt ends with the same style block.

When you send the images back, I check each one for:
- resolution (at least 1600 px on the long side)
- a plain white background
- stray text, letters or numbers
- accuracy against your notes

Accepted images go into the site as compressed WebP with a "what to notice" caption. Rejected ones get an exact "keep this image, change only X" fix.

**Style block (identical on every prompt):**
> Style: clean modern medical illustration, soft semi-realistic shading, muted clinical palette of deep blood reds, warm skin tones, pale straw yellow for plasma and teal accents, even studio lighting. One single illustration only, never a grid, collage or contact sheet. At least 1600 pixels on the long side. Plain pure white background with no checkerboard pattern and no shadow box. The whole subject is fully visible with generous empty margins; nothing is cut off at any edge. Absolutely no text, labels, letters, numbers, arrows with writing, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.
